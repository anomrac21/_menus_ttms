Add to Home Screen

Step 1 of 4

Tap Share in Safari

Open this menu in Safari (not Chrome or Instagram). Tap the Share button — a square with an arrow pointing up — in the toolbar at the bottom of the screen.

Step 2 of 4

Tap Add to Home Screen

In the share sheet, scroll down if needed, then tap Add to Home Screen.

Step 3 of 4

Tap Add

Check the name, then tap Add in the top right.

Step 4 of 4

Open the icon, then subscribe

Leave Safari and open {{ .Site.Title }} from your Home Screen. Tap Get menu alerts from that icon — Safari in a tab cannot enable them.
